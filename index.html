What is MAMG?

MAMG (Me And My Guys) is more than an event brand. It's a community built on friendship, connection and unforgettable experiences.

So, is MAMG just a party?

Nope! Parties are part of what we do, but MAMG is bigger than that. We create spaces for people to connect, vibe and build real memories together.

Who is MAMG for?

MAMG is for everyone who believes good experiences are better together. Your friends, your people and even the new ones you haven't met yet. If you like good vibes, you're already one of us.

Why “Me and My Guys”?

Because life is better when we do it together. MAMG is about the people you roll with, the bond you share and the memories you create.

What does MAMG actually do?

We create experiences that bring people together: raves, day parties, hangouts, pop-ups, trips and more. It's not just about the event, it's about the impact.

What kind of experiences can I expect?

Good vibes. Good people. Good music. Real connections. Every MAMG experience is curated to make sure you leave with stories worth telling and people worth calling your own.
